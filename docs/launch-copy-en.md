# Launch copy

Meet Harvard Veritas — The Living Archive.

I built an interactive 3D journey that moves from Harvard's campus into a living archive, where books arrive, pass through an illuminated scanner and find their place among readers and collections. The experience ends with six interactive volumes and 36 pages of stories, photographs and primary sources.

Built with React, Three.js, React Three Fiber and a free MapLibre/OpenFreeMap campus map. The latest update refines forklift clearances, adds an English interface, and reduces the opening film and model downloads for mobile visitors.

Visual studies by Vedant Hegde and Alex Bender / Fancy informed the material and lighting direction. This is an independent creative study, with no institutional affiliation with Harvard University.
